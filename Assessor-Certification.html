What is an Assessor Qualification?

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

These qualifications authorise you to make decisions about a learner's occupational competence.

  • 🔎 You gather evidence (observation, questions, work products).
  • ⚖️ You judge that evidence against a set of standards.
  • ✅ You provide feedback and sign off on their competence.

The most comprehensive qualification is the Level 3 CAVA. 🏆

The CAVA Qualification

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

CAVA stands for Certificate in Assessing Vocational Achievement.

This is the modern replacement for the old "A1" or "D32/D33" awards. It is the "gold standard" because it qualifies you to assess in ALL environments:

  • 🏗️ The Workplace: Real jobs, live tasks.
  • 🏫 The Learning Environment: Classrooms, workshops, simulations.

What does CAVA Cover?

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

To achieve CAVA, you typically complete three units:

  • 📘 Unit 1: Understanding the Principles & Practices of Assessment (Theory).
  • 💼 Unit 2: Assessing Competence in the Work Environment (Practical).
  • 🎓 Unit 3: Assessing Vocationally Related Achievement (Classroom/Skills Lab).

This makes you a fully qualified assessor for Apprenticeships and NVQs. 🌟

The ACWE Award

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

ACWE stands for Award in Assessing Competence in the Work Environment.

This is a shorter qualification for those who ONLY assess staff doing their daily jobs.

  • 🏗️ Ideal for: Construction site foremen assessing bricklayers.
  • ✂️ Ideal for: Salon managers assessing junior stylists on real clients.
  • ❌ You cannot assess in a classroom or workshop with this award.

The AVRA Award

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

AVRA stands for Award in Assessing Vocationally Related Achievement.

This is for assessors who ONLY work in learning environments, such as colleges or training centres.

  • 🏫 Ideal for: Lecturers marking assignments or tests.
  • 📝 Ideal for: Assessing simulations or role-plays (not real work).
  • ❌ You cannot assess a learner in their real place of work with this.

Do You Have the Experience?

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

You cannot buy these qualifications off the shelf—you need Occupational Competence. 🛠️

  • 💡 To assess a plumber, you must be a qualified plumber.
  • 💡 To assess a coder, you must have coding experience.

Regulatory bodies require you to have relevant, up-to-date industry experience to judge others safely and fairly. 📜

What happened to A1 and D32/33?

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

You might hear older colleagues refer to their "A1 Assessor Award" or the even older "D32/D33".

  • 🔄 These are legacy qualifications.
  • ✅ If you hold them, they are usually still valid (you don't need to retake CAVA).
  • 📈 However, you must show you have kept your skills up to date (CPD).

CAVA is simply the current version of these historic awards. 🕰️

The Assessment Cycle

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

Regardless of which qualification you choose (CAVA, ACWE, AVRA), you will learn the Assessment Cycle:

  1. 📅 Plan: Agree on what will be assessed and how.
  2. 👁️ Judge: Observe the learner or examine their work.
  3. 💬 Feedback: Give constructive praise and advice.
  4. 📝 Record: Document the decision formally.

How Will You Assess?

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

During your Level 3 course, you will master various methods:

  • 👀 Observation: Watching the learner work.
  • 🗣️ Professional Discussion: Asking them "why" they did something.
  • 📂 Product Evidence: Looking at documents or items they created.
  • ✍️ Witness Testimony: Asking their manager for feedback.

A good assessor uses a mix of these methods (Triangulation). 📐

Study Tips for Success

Level 3 assessor qualification guidance presented in a clear training format.

If you are enrolling in CAVA, prepare by:

  • 🤝 Finding two learners: You need real people to assess to pass your course.
  • 📑 Knowing the Standards: Read the NVQ/Unit criteria you are assessing against.
  • 🛡️ Understanding IQA: Learn how Internal Quality Assurers check your work.

Remember: Assessment must be Valid, Authentic, Current, Sufficient, and Reliable (VACSR). 🔐
