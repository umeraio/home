What is this page for?

Cybersecurity means protecting computers, phones, networks, websites, accounts, and data from damage, theft, misuse, and attack. This learning hub is designed for beginners, students, teachers, staff, small businesses, and anyone who wants to understand cybersecurity in simple English.

You do not need to be an expert. Start with the first topic, then move forward. Each section explains the idea, gives examples, and shows why it matters in real life.

Cybersecurity Learning Path

Use these 12 sections as your roadmap. They start with simple ideas and gradually move into more professional cybersecurity areas.

🛡️

1. Introduction to Cybersecurity

Understand what cybersecurity is, why it matters, and how cyber attacks affect real people and organisations.

🔐

2. Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Learn the basic language of security: risk, threats, vulnerabilities, CIA triad, authentication, and authorization.

🌐

3. Networking Fundamentals

Learn how computers talk to each other using IP addresses, DNS, DHCP, ports, protocols, and the OSI model.

💻

4. Operating Systems & Security

Understand Windows, Linux, users, permissions, updates, hardening, and how systems can be protected.

⚠️

5. Cyber Attacks & Threats

Learn about malware, ransomware, phishing, social engineering, DDoS, insider threats, and common attack methods.

🧱

6. Defensive Security

Learn how organisations defend systems using firewalls, antivirus, monitoring, logging, IDS, IPS, EDR, and SIEM.

🧪

7. Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing

Understand legal security testing, reconnaissance, scanning, enumeration, basic exploitation, and reporting.

🔎

8. Digital Forensics & Incident Response

Learn how incidents are investigated using evidence collection, timelines, IOCs, containment, and reporting.

☁️

9. Cloud & Modern Security

Learn about Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, SaaS security, MFA, Zero Trust, and cloud protection basics.

👥

10. Security Awareness & Human Factors

Understand passwords, email safety, scams, human error, social engineering, and security culture.

🧰

11. Cybersecurity Tools & Downloads

Explore common tools such as Wireshark, Nmap, Sysinternals, Burp Suite, Autopsy, scripts, and templates.

🎓

12. Certifications & Career Pathways

Learn about Security+, CEH, CISSP, SOC Analyst, DFIR Analyst, Blue Team, and career roadmaps.
